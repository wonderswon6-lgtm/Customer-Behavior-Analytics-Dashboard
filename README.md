# 📁 Data Analyst Projects

A collection of end-to-end data analytics projects covering retail, supply chain, and telecom domains — built using Power BI, SQL, Python, DAX, and Excel.

---

## 🗂️ Projects

### 1. 🛍️ [Customer Behavior Analytics Dashboard](./data%20analyst%20projects/customer%20behavior%20analysis/)
> Retail Analytics | Power BI · SQL · DAX · Excel

Analyzes customer shopping behavior across 3,900 transactions to uncover purchasing patterns, product preferences, customer demographics, and subscription trends. Delivers actionable insights to support marketing strategy and customer segmentation.

---

### 2. 🏭 [Smytten Supply Chain Analysis](./data%20analyst%20projects/smytten%20supply%20chain%20analysis/)
> Supply Chain | Power BI · Excel · PowerPoint

End-to-end supply chain analysis for Smytten, a D2C beauty & personal care platform, covering 100 SKUs across product performance, supplier evaluation, logistics efficiency, and defect tracking. Completed as a client project during a Datamites internship.

---

### 3. 📊 [Telco IBM Customer Churn Dashboard](./data%20analyst%20projects/telco%20IBM/)
> Telecom Analytics | Power BI · DAX · Python · MySQL · Excel

Interactive Power BI dashboard built on the IBM Telco Customer Churn Dataset to analyze churn patterns, identify key churn drivers, measure business impact, and support data-driven customer retention strategies.
