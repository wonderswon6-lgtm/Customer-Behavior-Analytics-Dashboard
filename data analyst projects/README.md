# 📁 Data Analyst Projects

A collection of end-to-end data analytics projects covering retail, supply chain, and telecom domains — built using Power BI, SQL, Python, DAX, and Excel.

---

## 🗂️ Projects

### 1. 🛍️ Customer Behavior Analytics Dashboard
> Retail analytics | Power BI · SQL · DAX · Excel

An end-to-end analytics workflow analyzing customer shopping behavior across 3,900 transactions. Uncovers purchasing patterns, customer demographics, product preferences, and subscription trends through an interactive Power BI dashboard.

**Highlights:**
- Customer segmentation and demographic analysis
- Product performance and subscription adoption trends
- Actionable insights for marketing and business strategy

### 2. 🏭 Smytten Supply Chain Analysis
> Supply chain | Power BI · Excel · PowerPoint

An end-to-end supply chain analysis for Smytten — a D2C beauty & personal care platform — covering 100 SKUs across product performance, supplier evaluation, logistics efficiency, and defect tracking. Completed as a client project during a Datamites internship.

**Highlights:**
- Supplier performance scorecard across 5 suppliers
- Defect rate trends and quality inspection analysis
- Shipping cost and route efficiency breakdown

### 3. 📊 Telco IBM Customer Churn Dashboard
> Telecom analytics | Power BI · DAX · Python · MySQL · Excel

An interactive Power BI dashboard built on the IBM Telco Customer Churn Dataset to analyze churn patterns, identify key churn drivers, measure business impact, and support data-driven retention strategies.

**Highlights:**
- Executive overview with dynamic KPI cards
- Churn driver analysis with drill-down and drill-through
- Customer 360° profile analysis page
